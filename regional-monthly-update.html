How We Report It
Pipeline & targets + monthly overheads
OKR progress with a realistic view of what is achievable vs AOP — plus OpEx tracked against budget per branch
Operational & financial health
Cash in/out, billing vs collection, and capital position — the numbers that show whether capacity is funded and sustainable
Operations & logistics + escalations
Productivity, supply chain, outsource-vs-HQ calls — closing with the decisions we need from you
💡
Why go deeper than P&L: a branch can show a healthy P&L while receivables pile up and cash drains. In EPCC, billing milestones and collection lag are where projects quietly bleed. Cash flow tells us the truth about balance sheet health — P&L alone does not.
