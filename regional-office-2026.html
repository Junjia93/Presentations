Context · Why Change Is Needed

The Current Model Creates
Accountability Gaps

01
Fragmented Reporting Lines
Country Managers simultaneously answer to multiple C-level BU Leaders — Operations, Strategy, Finance, and Sales — with no unified point of coordination.
02
Competing BU Priorities
Each BU Leader naturally prioritises their own vertical. Without a regional integrator, country teams receive conflicting direction and there is no one accountable for the full regional picture.
03
No Regional Line of Sight
Senior management lacks a consolidated, cross-country view of pipeline, operations, and risks. Reporting flows upward in silos, making strategic regional decisions reactive rather than proactive.
MD's Directive: A dedicated Regional Office has been approved to serve as the single point of accountability across all four Southeast Asian markets — providing both operational oversight and a unified channel to senior management.
