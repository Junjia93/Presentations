Context · Why Change Is Needed
The Current Model
Has Accountability Gaps
Country Managers currently navigate four separate C-level stakeholders with no unified regional point of accountability.
🔀
Problem 01
Fragmented Reporting Lines
Country Managers simultaneously answer to COO (Operations), CSO (Strategy), CFO (Finance), and VP (Sales). Each C-level pulls focus in a different direction with no unified coordination point.
⚡
Problem 02
Competing BU Priorities
Each BU Leader naturally prioritises their own vertical and KPIs. Country teams receive conflicting direction. There is no single person accountable for the full regional picture.
🔭
Problem 03
No Regional Line of Sight
Senior management lacks a consolidated view of pipeline, operations, and risks across all four markets. Information flows upward in silos — decisions become reactive rather than proactive.
🎯
MD's Directive — The Solution
The Managing Director has approved a dedicated Regional Office as the single point of accountability across Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, and Indonesia — providing both operational oversight and a unified channel to senior management. The Regional Office is not a filter that slows decisions. It is the regional integrator that enables faster, better-informed decisions.

Engagement Policy
In-Person
Is the Standard
Solarvest is in the business of trust. In Southeast Asia, relationships are built face-to-face — not over video calls. The Regional Office will represent Solarvest in person at every meaningful engagement.
4
Markets where the Regional Office represents Solarvest in person
What "In-Person First" Means in Practice
🤝
Client Meetings & Project Engagements
Regional Office attends key client meetings — project kick-offs, milestone reviews, relationship meetings, and issue resolution. No more remote-only representation for significant engagements.
🏛️
Government & Regulatory Engagements
Regional Office represents Solarvest in government, regulatory, and policy discussions across all four markets. Builds long-term institutional relationships that country teams can leverage.
🌏
Partner & JV Stakeholder Meetings
For joint ventures, investor relations, and strategic partner engagements — the Regional Office is the Solarvest face at the table, ensuring consistency of message and relationship continuity.
Policy Statement
All significant Solarvest engagements in the region will be conducted in person by the Regional Office. Virtual attendance is the exception, not the default.
