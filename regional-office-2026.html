Context & Background
Where We Stand Today
What is already in place — and what remains to be worked out as a company.
✅
Approved by MD
Regional Office is established
The Managing Director has formally approved the creation of the Regional Office to provide unified oversight across Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, and Indonesia. This is done — not under discussion today.
🔄
Transition Pending
Country managers aware — but still in old structure
Country managers have been informed that the reporting structure is changing. However, many are still operating under the old hierarchy — reporting to separate C-level stakeholders — as the formal transition has not yet been executed on the ground.
🏗️
Important Context
This is Solarvest's first major reorganisation. Many structural questions — reporting lines, authority thresholds, HR processes, operating rhythms — have not yet been formally discussed. A consultant will be engaged to guide the company through the transition (not yet identified). Today begins those conversations.

Discussion Topic
Reporting Line
Transition
The structure is approved. The question now is: how do we actually make the change happen on the ground?
✓ Reporting Line Approved by MD
Current Reality
Country managers in overseas offices are still operating under the old hierarchy — reporting to separate C-level stakeholders. The gap between the approved structure and actual practice needs to be closed.
Open Discussion
Who should own and drive the transition plan — and what does the plan look like?
⚖️
Regional Office should not drive this As the direct beneficiary of the restructure, Regional Office taking ownership of the transition creates a conflict of interest and potential bias. A neutral party should lead.
🏛️
Consultant to be engaged Solarvest will engage an external consultant to guide the transition. The consultant will help structure the process objectively. Identity not yet confirmed.
💬
Questions for the room Who communicates the change to C-levels? Who handles the HR implications? What is the timeline? Who owns the announcement to country teams?
📋
HR involvement is critical Country manager KPIs, performance reviews, and reporting structures in HR systems all need to reflect the new structure. HR needs to be looped in early.
02
Discussion Topic
Level of
Authority
The current LOA has no Regional Office tier. We are proposing a revision — specifically for EPCC and O&M agreements.
Scope of Discussion
Focused on operational agreements — EPCC contracts and O&M agreements for regional markets. Two options are on the table. Which direction does senior management prefer?
Two Options to Explore
Option 1 · Delegation Model
E&O CEO retains signing authority — Regional Office assists & delegates to speed up the process
E&O CEO continues to sign EPCC and O&M agreements. Regional Office person-in-charge is delegated to understand the details, support the workflow, and act as the liaison to accelerate signing.
✦ Maintains existing authority chain · Less structural disruption · Faster to implement
Option 2 · B&A Authority Model
B&A CEO has full authority over regional operations including EPCC & O&M signing
The CEO of Business & Asset is granted authority to decide on regional operations — including signing EPCC and O&M agreements for all four Southeast Asian markets.
✦ Cleaner authority line · Aligns with new reporting structure · Empowers B&A vertical
Discussion question: Which option better reflects how Solarvest wants to govern regional operations? Are there hybrid arrangements worth exploring?

04
Discussion Topic
Bonus
Mechanism
A brief discussion on whether Regional Office can have the mandate to propose a new performance reward system for country teams.
Why This Matters
Motivating overseas teams during a period of structural change is critical. Without a performance incentive structure, driving alignment and results across four markets becomes much harder.
Background & Context
❌
~2 Years Ago
BU Reward System — Discontinued
Solarvest previously had a Business Unit reward system to motivate overseas teams. This has since been discontinued and is no longer in place.
💡
Proposed
Regional Office Right to Propose
Can the Regional Office be granted the right to propose a new performance-based reward mechanism to top management — for country teams that perform well against agreed targets?
Discussion Question
Should Regional Office have the mandate to design and propose a bonus or reward mechanism to top management on behalf of the country teams?
